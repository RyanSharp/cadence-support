About Cadence

Cadence is a recurring task reminder app for iOS. Instead of picking a fixed date, Cadence tracks tasks based on when you last completed them — so your due dates always reflect reality.

Create tasks with a repeat interval, mark them complete, attach notes and photos, and get notified before things are due.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • How does recurrence work? When you mark a task complete, its next due date is calculated from that moment — not a fixed calendar date.
  • Where is my data stored? All data is stored locally on your device. No account is required.
  • How do I attach photos to a completion? When marking a task complete, tap "Add Photos" in the completion sheet. Photos are stored in iCloud and require an iCloud account.
  • How do I enable notifications? Tap the gear icon on the task list, select "Per Task", and choose how many days before the due date you'd like to be notified.
  • How do I delete a task? Swipe left on any task in the list and tap Delete.
