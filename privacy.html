Cadence

Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 15, 2026

Overview

Cadence is designed with privacy in mind. All of your task data is stored locally on your device or in your own private iCloud account. No data is sent to any server operated by the developer, and no analytics or tracking of any kind is collected.

Data Stored on Your Device

Cadence stores the following data locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework:

  • Your name (entered during onboarding, used in completion records)
  • Task names, instructions, and repeat intervals
  • Completion history, including dates, notes, and duration
  • Notification preferences

This data never leaves your device except as described below under iCloud.

iCloud Photo Storage

When you attach photos to a task completion, those photos are stored in your own private iCloud database using Apple's CloudKit framework. This means:

  • Photos are stored in your personal iCloud account, not on any developer-operated server
  • The developer has no access to your photos or any iCloud data
  • iCloud storage is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy

Photo storage requires an active iCloud account. If you do not use this feature, no iCloud data is created.

Notifications

Cadence uses local push notifications only. Notifications are scheduled on your device and do not involve any external server or network request. No notification content is transmitted to the developer.

Analytics & Tracking

Cadence does not use any analytics, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs. No usage data, identifiers, or behavioral information is collected or shared with any third party.

Data Deletion

To delete all local data, simply delete the Cadence app from your device. To remove photos stored in iCloud, go to iCloud settings and manage your CloudKit data, or delete the app while iCloud sync is enabled.

Children's Privacy

Cadence does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. The app does not include any features directed at children.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy changes in a meaningful way, the updated policy will be posted here with a new effective date.
